About

Welcome to Happy Home, a Roblox animated competition series where teams face exciting challenges, unexpected twists, and intense battles to become the ultimate winners.

Contestants enter Happy Home with one goal: to work together, survive every challenge, and claim the grand prize. But with every competition comes new obstacles, rivalries, and moments that test how far each team is willing to go.

Follow the journey as teams compete, friendships are made, and every episode brings a new challenge closer to the final victory.
